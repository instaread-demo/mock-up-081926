      The history of Australian infrastructure: Growth (1947-1968)

      PART 3 | War showed what Australia could build when resources, industry, and government aligned. After 1945, that capacity had to serve national development.
      Ian Fitzgerald
      Aug 19, 2026 7 min read

      Share

      Infrastructure entered a new phase in which nation-building became an exercise in prioritisation.

      “Our resources will be developed, not wasted; first things will be put first; and the overall economy of Australia will be brought into better balance.” (Menzies, 1951)

      War had proved Australia could build at scale. The post-war challenge was deciding what should be built first. Faced with competing demands for housing, power, transport and industrial development, Robert Menzies argued that public investment should be coordinated, sequenced and aligned with national priorities.
